Tom Holland

Thomas Stanley Holland, known professionally as Tom Holland, is an English actor and dancer. He is widely known for playing Peter Parker / Spider-Man in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

Tom Holland

British actor best known for playing Spider-Man.

Introduction

Thomas Stanley Holland (born 1 June 1996) is a British actor known for his work in a diverse range of genres. His accolades include a BAFTA Award as well as two Critics Choice Awards nominations. Holland's films as a leading actor have grossed over $13.1 billion worldwide, making him one of the highest-grossing actors of all time. He was featured on the Forbes 30 Under 30 Europe list of 2019.

Early Life

Tom Holland was born on June 1, 1996, in Kingston upon Thames, London, England. He grew up in a creative family and developed an interest in performing at a young age.

Holland was educated at Donhead Preparatory School, an all-male Catholic preparatory school in Wimbledon, in the Merton borough of south west London.When he was seven, he was diagnosed with dyslexia. His parents sent him and his brothers (to avoid making them feel neglected) to a private school so he could get the necessary attention.Although Holland liked the new school, this started to drain his family's finances. Holland attended Wimbledon College, a voluntary aided Jesuit comprehensive school,followed by the BRIT School for Performing Arts and Technology in the Selhurst area of Croydon.

Film Career

Tom Holland made his major film debut in The Impossible in 2012. His performance received positive attention and helped him establish himself as a young actor.

In The Impossible, Holland played Lucas, a young boy who survives a devastating natural disaster with his family.

Spider-Man Career

In 2015, Tom Holland was announced as the new actor to play Peter Parker / Spider-Man in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

He first appeared as Spider-Man in Captain America: Civil War in 2016. His young and energetic portrayal of Peter Parker became popular among audiences.
