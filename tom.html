Tom Holland

About Tom Holland

Tom Holland is a British actor and dancer. He is best known for playing Spider-Man in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

Early Life

Tom Holland was born on June 1, 1996, in Kingston upon Thames, England. He started his career as a performer and later became a successful actor.

Career

Tom Holland began his career in theatre and gained recognition for Billy Elliot the Musical. He later became famous around the world for playing Spider-Man.
